Privacy Policy

Mee-Mee Road (めぇめぇロード) / Last updated: 1 November 2026

Katsumi Sakurai ("we") provides this policy for the iOS app "Mee-Mee Road" (the "App").

1. Information we do not collect

The App requires no account and we run no server of our own. We never receive any information about you.

The App does not use or collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.

2. Data stored on your device

Only the following is stored on your device (iOS UserDefaults). It is never sent anywhere.

TypeContents
Best scoreYour highest score
Top 10The scores of your ten best games and when you played them

Deleting the App removes all of it. We cannot read or retrieve any of it.

3. Advertising

The App shows third-party advertising through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect:

TypeContents
Advertising identifiersIDFA and identifiers issued by AdMob
Device informationDevice model, OS version, language, screen size, etc.
Coarse locationCountry/region estimated from the IP address
Ad interactionImpressions, taps, etc.
DiagnosticsCrash and performance information

This data is collected and used by Google; we do not receive it. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

A banner ad appears at the bottom of the game screen, and a full-screen ad may appear when a game ends — at most once every five minutes. A rewarded video ad is shown only when you choose "広告を見てつづける" (watch an ad to continue) after getting stuck. No full-screen ad is ever shown while you are placing pieces.

4. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On first launch the App may show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. The advertising identifier is used for personalised advertising only if you choose "Allow". If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you will see non-personalised ads and every feature of the App works exactly the same.

You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. Children

The App is not designed for children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when laws change or features are added. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
